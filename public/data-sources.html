A whole world to explore

Our 197-country pool includes the 193 UN members, Palestine, Vatican City, Kosovo and Taiwan. Dependent territories are not separate quiz entries. Each trip draws a fresh selection from the full pool.

Three clues per country

The original 12 countries keep their cultural and nature clues. The expanded set adds geographic and language clues. Languages are examples of languages spoken, not exhaustive lists.

Country data

Adapted from mledoze/countries, with updates and more specific questions for countries with multiple capital roles. The modified database is available in the game repository under the Open Database License.

The border map

Public-domain Natural Earth borders, packaged by world-atlas. The map generally follows de facto boundaries; inclusion and borders do not imply a position on recognition. Flights focus on a country’s largest landmass. A ring helps locate tiny countries.
